Revision 3 is thorough and almost ready to build. I checked the code references against the real files (Projectiles.luau:34 wallParams, Abilities.clearPath around line 190, Enemies.luau:346 shotReady and :406 shoot, the :460 summons, FinaleFight.luau:139, the World/Waves spawn flow, Movement.findAnchor, the Config notice ranges) and the file line references are correct. The Rail/ShotRay collision-group approach works. The toss and reach fallback plugs into Rules.aiStep correctly, since shotReady is checked before reach. Part and light budgets are well under the caps. I also spot-checked pads and footprints in Times Square, Quarry and Thunderspire; the published gaps hold, apart from the transit shelter's missing yaw.

It is not ready to build as written. Four things must be fixed first:
- **Gaps at deck and wall joints.** Chord decks and wall flats were sized to the centre line, which leaves holes where pieces meet: up to 1.9 u at the Colony overpass joints and 1.28 u cracks at the Kyoto wall corners. The Vault gallery and Tideline deck lengths are not given at all.
- **spawnSpot code.** It sorts pads by least-recently-used and then shuffles, which throws the sort away. It also calls a `Rules.shuffled` function that does not exist.
- **Kyoto gate stairs.** They run parallel to the wall with no top landing, and their high end opens onto a drop at the gate gap.
- **Terrain hills.** A random hill can start at r 56, which clashes with the Thunderspire stair foot at x 58 and the Dunewatch masts at 58.

There are also two naming contradictions (THEME_BUDGET vs Layouts.budget, and TOSS vs Nav.toss) and yaws missing for pieces described only as 'rotated'.

Improvements:
- Physics shows swinging cannot put a player on the 20–40 u perches; relabel them as flight-only or give them a real access.
- Keep tree crowns queryable. In my offline run of the real 45-ray fan, this gives many more grid points full swing coverage.
- Port the real fan into the Lune spec in place of the distance rule.
- Add crossarms to thin masts so the swing rays can hit them.
- Make the Academy hero sightline rule explicit about rails.
- Give the finale Seam a proper hero set piece.

All of these are small, precise edits. With them the design would be excellent.

## Blocking

1. Chord decks and wall segments leave V-shaped holes where they meet, because each rectangle's length was sized to the centre line instead of the outer edge. (a) Colony overpass: the decks are 17.2 u long at r 44, but the outer edge (r 48) needs 2·48·tan(11.25°) = 19.1 u. Every one of the 7 joints has a gap of 0.3 u at the centre line that widens to 1.9 u (4.5 studs) at the outer rail, and a character can drop through it. Either make each deck 19.1 u long so neighbours overlap on the inner side, or add a 6 × 8 × 0.5 pier cap over each of the 9 piers at h 6.5–7 (+9 parts). Pier caps also read better. (b) Kyoto octagon: the flats are 44.7 u (= 2·54·tan 22.5°), so each outer corner has a crack 1.28 u wide that runs down the full 6.5 u height and notches the wall-walk. Either make the flats 46.0 u (= 2·55.5·tan 22.5°), or put a 4 × 4 × 8 corner bastion at each of the 8 vertices (apothem-54 vertex radius 58.45). Bastions give +8 parts and a much better silhouette. (c) Star Vault gallery (12 decks at r 49–55) and Mistreach Tideline (16 chords at r 55–60) give no length. Set them to 29.5 u (= 2·55·tan 15°) and 23.9 u (= 2·60·tan 11.25°). Add a check to layouts.spec: along each walk, sample at the outer edge and require support under every sample.
2. Waves.spawnSpot code is wrong as written. It sorts `all` by least-recently-used, then iterates `Rules.shuffled(pool)`, which throws the ordering away. `Rules.shuffled` also does not exist in src/shared/Rules.luau ('or a local Fisher-Yates' is not a spec). The fix: add a local `shuffle(t)` (in-place Fisher-Yates using math.random). For fresh pools, shuffle. For the `all` fallback, iterate in the sorted LRU order and do not shuffle. Keep `used` keyed per build. Also say explicitly that the `centre` argument is ignored when Nav exists, and that `Waves.spawnCurrent` must compute `boss` before calling (it already has `Worlds.enemy(id).boss` on line 94).
3. The Kyoto main-gate stair flights (x ±(4..15.4), z 49.5..52.5, rise 6.5) are tangential with no landing. Their high end points at x ±4, which is open air in front of the 8 u gate gap. A player walking up drops off the top unless they turn north in the last ~1.5 u. Use the same fix as the other tangential ramps: move each flight to x ±(7..18.4) and add a 3 × 3 landing at x ±(4..7), z 49.5..52.5, h 6.5, flush with the wall-walk and gatehouse deck. Put a rail on the landing's gate-facing (inner) edge. Check that the torii and pads remain clear afterwards.
4. Terrain hills conflict with structures beyond r 56. `hill()` puts the ground intersection at ≥ 56 u (d ≥ 56 + 0.8r with a −0.6r sink). The storm theme calls hill() on line 951, and the Thunderspire ledge stair's Ramp 1 starts at x 58 (r ≈ 58.2), so a random hill can bury the ramp foot or bulge through the pinnacle stair. Dunewatch (desert, line 747) needs the same check against its caravan masts at (±12,−58). Give hill() a per-theme minimum ground radius: storm 66, desert 66, earth 60. Also add a Layouts `keepOut` list of {bearing, halfWidth} sectors that hills reject. Add a test that no hill footprint (d − 0.8r) is closer than max footprint radius + 4.
5. Two self-contradictions a developer would trip on: (1) §3 says `THEME_BUDGET` 370/20 and similar, and §4.1 warns against `THEME_BUDGET[theme]`, but that table does not exist in Arenas.luau and the schema in §4.5 defines `Layouts[theme].budget`. Pick one: use `Layouts[theme].budget` and delete THEME_BUDGET. (2) B6 says `local prof = e.t.ranged or TOSS`, while §4.3 says `e.t.ranged or Nav.toss(e)`. Specify `Nav.toss(e)`, which returns a fresh table with power = Rules.round(e.t.atk*0.5), so it never mutates a shared TOSS the way Combat.luau:456 mutates `e.t.ranged.cd`.
6. Times Square transit shelters: '4 × 2 × 3 at (24, 8) and (8, 24), rotated' gives no yaw. At 45° the (24, 8) shelter reaches x 26.1, which is inside pad (32, 11)'s clear box (x 26..38). Give the exact yaw (e.g. 0° or 90°, whichever the offline model used). Also give yaws for every other piece listed only as 'rotated' or 'tilted' (the Leaning Block's 6° tilt axis, and the Seam FillBlocks).
