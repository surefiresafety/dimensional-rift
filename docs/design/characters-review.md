# Reviewer's outstanding items after round 3 (score 7/10)

Revision 2 is strong and mostly grounded in the real code. I checked its line references against the files: Rules.luau:389/410/488, Enemies.luau:385/423/426, Combat.luau:280/284/416/669, Waves.luau:101, FinaleFight.luau:129/134, Abilities.luau possess and mahoraga. The cylinder, ellipsoid and weld conventions are correct Roblox. Alpha templates really are boss=true, the 46 styles and 11 floaters match the data, and most part sums check out (the Tempest Fox, Unbidden, Hyakunui and all creature variants are 30 or under). It still cannot ship as written. The biggest problem is that it inherits every unlisted Characters.luau colour and every data flag, and that data is franchise trade dress: yellow sleeves on Clawback, a red face on Threadrunner, gold sleeves on Arc Knight, cowl flags on Wolverine, red sig glows on Skyline bosses. The three highest-profile playables still echo their sources through hair colour: Aoi's pale hair contradicts the design's own §7, Akuro keeps near-pink spiky hair with four arms, and Ikazuchi moved toward Kashimo's blue. About 25 signature pieces have no host or offset, and the headphones land on the eyes. There are real geometry bugs: the glider is a horizontal tray through the chest, the Unbidden's side collar tiles are buried, the Gravewell orbit passes through the torso, the spike rotation sign sweeps hair forward, and the forelock fails test 10. Kit scaling would double-scale creatures and has no HRP entry. The Armored shell hides weak points and trimming drops them. The client death read fights the server fade in Combat.kill. The Suit listener would rebuild the costume on every hit. All of these are surgical fixes. With them, plus client-side sway for capes and tails, this would be an excellent, buildable design.

## Blocking

1. PALETTE AND FLAG LEAKAGE (IP). §3 says 'Colours not listed keep their Characters.luau values', and §2.7 turns data flags into kit pieces. Characters.luau is generated franchise data, so both rules bring the franchise looks back. Examples from the real file: naruto sleeves 0xf66c2d orange and sig orange (Ren would have orange arms under a teal torso); spiderman skin 0xd8323c (Threadrunner's face strip between the hood and LM would be red) and sig red; ironman skin red, sleeves 0xffc83d gold, sig gold; ironspider skin, top and bottom red with sig gold; miles sig 0xff2d3a and sandals red; carnage sig red; wolverine sleeves 0xd8a83a yellow (a yellow-armed Clawback), plus mask=true and horns=true, which map to FM+HN and rebuild the cowl; goblin skin green and hood=purple (HD flag); strange sash 0xd4b04a gold, buckle 0x6ad88a green, cape and collar red; docock goggles=true (GG) and hairStyle 'bowl'; kakashi headband (HB); itachi collar red (HC); sukuna markings (face marks). Character bosses use FaceGlow in the 'signature colour', which is data `sig`, so boss_spiderman and boss_miles would get red glowing slits. Fix: (a) state that for players and character bosses STYLE[id].kit is authoritative and flag→kit mapping runs only for EnemyLooks human enemies; (b) STYLE.recolor must set skin, hair, top, sleeves, bottom, eye, brow and sig for every Skyline style and for any style whose leftover data colour is franchise-coded; (c) rename the Costumes STYLE field `sig` (the signature kit entry) to `sigPiece` so it cannot collide with Characters.sig (a colour); (d) test 5 must check the EFFECTIVE merged look: all six HumanoidDescription limb colours, paintParts colours, sig, and every kit tint.
2. IP ON THE THREE BIGGEST-NAME PLAYABLES. Aoi Hakumei: §7 says the pale hair was removed, but §3 keeps hair 0xc8c0e0 (silver-lavender). With glowing cyan eyes, a navy academy coat and the Infinity/Void kit, that is two of the three Gojo tells. Change hair to deep slate 0x2a3448 with the two HR·w wedges in cyan-white 0xbfe8ff, so pale shows only as streaks. Akuro: hair 0xe8d8d0 is almost the data's 0xf7c6c6 pink. Spiky pale-pink hair plus four arms plus crimson is the Heian Sukuna silhouette. Change to ash-black 0x2a1a1c with ember tips 0x9b1a2a on the HR·s, and swap spikes for 2 HR·w swept wedges. Ikazuchi: 0xdfe8ff pale-blue long hair with a staff moves toward Kashimo, not away. Use 0xf0d27a storm-gold, or near-black 0x1e2230 with a yellow HR·p tip.
3. BESPOKE PIECES HAVE NO HOST OR OFFSET. §2.3 gives numbers only for the standard kit. Many signature pieces in §3 and §5 give a size but no host attachment or C0, so a developer has to invent the look-defining geometry. Missing: Top Hat brim, crown and band (e.g. brim Head (0,0.64,0), crown (0,1.14,0), band (0,0.72,0)); Kurobane and Karasu feather Wedges; Karasu clip; Thornbloom leaf; Genma orb and Rei wisp (e.g. UpperTorso (−1.3,1.3,+0.4), with an optional 0.6 rev/s Orbit); Akagane vials; Overtime tie and watch; Hammer Maiden hammer; Gankyu flat-top and cannon; Ikazuchi staff host; Patchwork shoulder plates and seam strip; Unbidden chest plates and shoulder domes; grey enemy eyes and part count; BA segment 2 and Eightfold's claw (need a chain rule: C0_k = C0_{k-1} * CFrame.new(0, L_{k-1}/2, 0) * rot_k * CFrame.new(0, L_k/2, 0)). Headphones reuse GG, whose only positions are over the eyes, so Nosebleed's cups land on her eyes. Give them Cyl Ø0.45×0.14 at Head (±0.64, 0.02, +0.05) with no rotation, and draw the band as a curved Beam between the cups (CurveSize0 = CurveSize1 = 0.9, 0 parts), because a straight 1.3 Block cannot reach from the cap top (y 0.77) down to the cups. Add a §3.1 table: code, size, host, attachment, C0, for each of these ~25 pieces.
4. GEOMETRY ERRORS. (1) WG glider is `CYL_UP*rx(-20)` at BodyBack: a flat horizontal disc Ø2.2 that runs through the chest like a tray. A back-mounted disc needs `CYL_FWD` (axis along Z) at BodyBack (0,0.1,+0.2). Only put it under the feet while flying (see improvements). (2) Unbidden collar: a circle of radius 0.78 on a 2×1 torso buries the 90° and 270° tiles inside the shoulders and the shoulder domes, and floats the 0° and 180° tiles 0.28 off the chest. Use a ring of radius 0.62 at NeckAttachment y +0.12 with each tile tipped rx(−20) outward (a gorget above the torso top), and move the domes out to ShoulderAttachment (±0.15,0.1,0) so they clear it. (3) `Orbit_*` rotates about the host centre, so Gravewell's r=0.9 axis-Z ring around the UpperTorso centre passes through the torso. Add an `OrbitCenter` attribute (Gravewell: (0,0.4,+1.1) in UpperTorso space). (4) Spike sign: by the design's own convention rx(θ<0) moves the TOP toward −Z, so HR·s `rx(-30)` and HR·w `rx(-15)` sweep hair FORWARD, while Ren and Kurobane specify 'swept back'. Use rx(+30) and rx(+15) for back-swept hair. (5) HR·f spans x −0.52..−0.32, which lies inside EYE_ZONE x ∈ [−0.45,0.45] with z −0.65, so test 10 fails on Karasu. Either narrow EYE_ZONE to x ∈ [−0.33,0.33] (the whites plus brows span −0.32..0.32) or add HR·f to the exempt set.
5. SCALING MODE IS UNDEFINED FOR CREATURES AND FINALE BUILDERS. `b:mount` always multiplies by host.Size/NOMINAL[host.Name]. Creature pieces are already sized in B, which includes t.size, and they weld to LowerTorso, which buildNpc has already scaled by size. That scales them twice (a 1.6 mass_curse would come out ×2.56). The Tempest Fox welds everything to HumanoidRootPart, which is not in NOMINAL, so the lookup is nil. Fix: add `opts.absolute=true` for CREATURES, BUILDERS, markers on HRP and SD, and add NOMINAL.HumanoidRootPart = (2,2,1).
6. MARKERS HIDE OR DROP WEAK POINTS. (a) The Armored motif is an opaque DiamondPlate shell at 1.08× the main body. It fully encloses the blob's transparent jelly and Neon core, the golem crack, and the human VS, emblem and lapels. Replace it with 2 DiamondPlate plates (humans: SG-sized pauldrons, 1.2×0.35×1.2 at the shoulders; creatures: 2 Wedges 0.5B×0.2B×0.6B on the top flanks). (b) The budget trims from the end, but toad warts, bat_curse and finger_bearer pieces sit at the end, so a toad elite (14 + ring + motif = 16) loses its wart cluster, which is its weak point. Mount weak-point pieces immediately after markers, and have test 3 assert they survive. (c) The Volatile motif is 'weak point pulses', but human enemies have no weak point, so a Volatile human shows only a ring. Add a Neon fuse Ball Ø0.35 at BodyBack (0,0.6,+0.2) for humans.
7. DEATH READ FIGHTS THE SERVER. Combat.kill (Combat.luau:357-363) already tweens every visible BasePart's Transparency to 1 over 1.2 s on the server. That replicates and overwrites the client's Neon flash and 0.4 s fade, which the design calls 0 server cost. The kill burst at Combat.luau:346 also uses `e.t.color`, which is the affix colour after applyAffix (F10), so the §6.2 step 7 fix is incomplete. Fix: store `e.pal` at spawn, have kill use `e.pal.base`, and have kill skip descendants of the `Costume` folder (the client owns their fade), or drop the client fade and keep only the 0.1 s Neon flash.
8. SUIT LISTENER REBUILDS ON EVERY HIT. Combat.publish (Combat.luau:280) rewrites `Suit` = floor(suitHp) every 0.1 s, so it changes on every hit. §6.2 says `Looks.redress` handles 'Suit changes', which would destroy and rebuild the whole costume and FP on each hit and flicker. Make it edge-triggered: cache `hasSuit = (Suit or 0) > 0` and only mount or destroy the two TN pieces when that boolean flips. Redress only on Weapon keys that change the suit (ironman).
9. ARITHMETIC: Threadrunner is written 1+3+1+2+1+2 = 12, but that sums to 10. Either the wrist spools are separate from GL (then list 'GL×2 + spools×2' and the total is 12, or 14 with the suit), or it is 10 (12 with the suit). Fix the row so test 2 matches.
